# Flipkart Sales Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Flipkart sales data to understand overall sales performance, profitability, product categories, regional contributions, customer segments, and payment methods.

The dataset contains **1,000 recorded transactions** across **5 categories, 4 regions, 6 payment methods, and 3 customer segments**.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify the top-performing product categories
- Compare regional sales and profitability
- Analyze customer segments
- Understand payment-method performance
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Identify products with high sales contribution
- Generate insights to support inventory, marketing, and business decisions

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Excel

## 📈 Key Analysis

The project includes analysis of:

- Category-wise Sales & Discount
- Region-wise Sales & Profit
- Monthly Sales Performance
- Segment-wise Sales
- Category-wise Sales & Customer Rating
- Top Products by Sales
- Payment Method Analysis

## 🔑 Key Insights

- Electronics is the strongest-performing category.
- Home & Kitchen has the strongest customer rating.
- North is the most profitable region.
- Wallet and Net Banking are important payment channels.
- Wholesale customers generate the highest revenue.

## 📌 Business Objective

The analysis can help businesses make informed decisions regarding:

- Inventory planning
- Regional advertising
- Product promotions
- Customer segmentation
- Payment-channel strategies
- Quarterly growth planning

## 📊 Dashboard

The project includes a sales performance dashboard presenting key metrics, category analysis, regional performance, monthly trends, customer segments, and product-level insights.
